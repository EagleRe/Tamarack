01 The house

Single-story living on a third-acre corner.

8367 Tamarack Drive is a 1,211 sq ft ranch built in 1985 on a level 0.30-acre corner lot, in an established Florence neighborhood just off Weaver Road. Everything is on one floor: three bedrooms, two full baths and an attached two-car garage.

The living room sits under a vaulted ceiling, anchored by a floor-to-ceiling brick fireplace and open to the dining area, with a pass-through to the galley kitchen. The kitchen has granite counters, oak cabinetry, a double stainless sink, and range, dishwasher and refrigerator in place. There is a separate laundry room off the kitchen and a walk-in closet beside the primary bath.

Outside: a concrete patio, a fenced backyard with a storage shed, and a hot tub on its own pad with a dedicated circuit.

The mechanicals are documented in the photos — a Rheem 50-gallon electric water heater installed March 2017, a Tempstar air handler built August 2018, and a labeled Square D panel carrying Kentucky electrical inspection certificates from 2020.
