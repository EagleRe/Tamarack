01 The house

Single-story living on a third-acre corner.

8367 Tamarack Drive is a 1,211 sq ft ranch built in 1985 on a level 0.30-acre corner lot, in an established Florence neighborhood just off Weaver Road. Everything is on one floor: three bedrooms, two full baths and an attached two-car garage.

The living room opens up under a vaulted ceiling and is anchored by a floor-to-ceiling brick wood-burning fireplace with a tile hearth. The galley kitchen has granite counters, oak cabinetry, a granite peninsula, a stainless range and dishwasher, and flows into a bright dining area with double French doors out to a concrete patio and the backyard.

Brick and vinyl exterior on a slab foundation, central air, electric forced-air heat, public water and sewer.
